# Electronics Foundations

Order, prerequisites and status live in `graph/concepts.json` (concepts `e01` to `e18`). This file holds the intent and the primary references.

## Intent

Build electronics from electromagnetism. Lessons 1 to 13 establish the field description (charge, E, potential, conduction, B, induction, Maxwell's equations, energy flow). Lesson 14 derives circuit theory as an approximation of it and states when the approximation fails. Lessons 15 to 18 use both views on real components and end at the buck converter, which opens the Power Electronics field.

## Primary references

- Feynman, Leighton, Sands, *The Feynman Lectures on Physics*, Vol. II (feynmanlectures.caltech.edu)
- Purcell & Morin, *Electricity and Magnetism*, 3rd ed.
- Griffiths, *Introduction to Electrodynamics*, 4th ed.
- MIT OCW 8.02 course notes (Liao, Dourmashkin, Belcher)
- Agarwal & Lang, *Foundations of Analog and Digital Electronic Circuits* (MIT 6.002), for the lumped-element abstraction
- Chabay & Sherwood, *Matter and Interactions*, for surface charge in circuits
- Sze, *Physics of Semiconductor Devices*; Sedra & Smith, for lessons 16 and 17
- Erickson & Maksimović, *Fundamentals of Power Electronics*, for lesson 18
