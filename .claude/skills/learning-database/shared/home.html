Learning Database

Physics and engineering built up from the governing laws, one 10 to 15 minute lesson at a time. Each field is one page; each lesson is a tab on it. Lessons derive their results, compute real numbers in their interactives, and cite the textbooks they follow.

Three ways to use it

With the Learning Database skill installed, ask Claude in any of these ways. Ask a deep question and you get a short answer plus the chain of lessons the full answer rests on. Name a concept you want to learn and Claude opens its lesson, writing it first if nobody has yet. Ask for the next lesson in a field and Claude opens the one after the last you finished.

Connections between fields

Where a concept in one field rests on, or reappears in, another. A title becomes a link once its lesson is written.
